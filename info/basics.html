Welcome to the Terrariums. This is the afterlife for all of your favorite characters.

The Basics:

All of your favorites are dead. That is how they arrived here. The default state you find them in will vary. If you ever want this to change, take them out of their room to "click" with another member so they may remember their bonds or create new ones. You may erase their memories after each play session. They are yours to play with. They cannot be hurt.

Each member in their default states in their room is a soft amalgamation of the most written times of their lives where they are the focus. For example: Plenty of Jimmy's story focuses on his admission and the soft, fluffy life he has with Fry. However, Fry's story focuses on his harbored feelings and his missed confessions in addition to more of the dramatic and steamy moments. Therefore, Fry in his default state is not officially romantically involved with Jimmy.
